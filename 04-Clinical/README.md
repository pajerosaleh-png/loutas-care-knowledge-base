# Clinical

## Overview

The Clinical domain defines the architecture, workflows, and governance for all clinical activities within the LOUTAS Care platform.

This section serves as the authoritative reference for the Electronic Medical Record (EMR), clinical encounters, documentation standards, patient journey, clinical orders, decision support, and all related clinical processes.

---

## Purpose

Provide a unified clinical architecture that ensures:

- Standardized clinical workflows.
- Consistent patient documentation.
- Safe clinical decision support.
- Structured medical records.
- Interoperability across all clinical modules.

---
