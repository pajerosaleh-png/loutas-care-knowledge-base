# FR-PHARM-001 — Pharmacy Overview & Medication Dispensing

**Document Classification:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** Critical  
**Status:** Approved Draft  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Business Objective

Provide a comprehensive pharmacy management framework that enables healthcare organizations to safely prescribe, dispense, track, and manage medications while ensuring patient safety, regulatory compliance, inventory synchronization, financial accuracy, and complete auditability.

The Pharmacy module shall support outpatient clinics, specialty centers, and future expansion to enterprise healthcare organizations.

---

# 2. Scope

This requirement governs the complete medication dispensing lifecycle, including:

- Prescription processing
- Medication verification
- Dispensing workflow
- Medication substitutions
- Controlled medication handling
- Patient counseling
- Inventory synchronization
- Billing integration
- Dispensing history

The Pharmacy module shall integrate seamlessly with EMR, Billing, Inventory, Reporting, Security, and Audit Trail modules.

---

# 3. Primary Actors

- Pharmacist
- Pharmacy Technician
- Physician
- Nurse (Read-Only where permitted)
- Billing Officer
- Inventory Manager
- System Administrator

---

# 4. Preconditions

The following conditions shall be satisfied before medication dispensing:

- User is authenticated.
- User has pharmacy permissions.
- Patient record exists.
- Valid prescription exists.
- Medication exists in the medication catalog.
- Medication is available in inventory unless organizational policy permits backorders.
- Required approvals have been completed for restricted medications.

---

# 5. Functional Requirements

## FR-PHARM-001.1 Prescription Retrieval

The system shall allow authorized pharmacy users to retrieve electronic prescriptions generated by the EMR.

Prescription information shall include:

- Patient Information
- Prescribing Physician
- Encounter Reference
- Medication List
- Dosage
- Frequency
- Route
- Duration
- Clinical Notes

---

## FR-PHARM-001.2 Prescription Validation

Before dispensing, the system shall validate:

- Prescription status.
- Patient identity.
- Medication availability.
- Prescription validity period.
- Duplicate dispensing prevention.
- Required approvals for controlled medications.

Validation failures shall prevent dispensing until resolved.

---

## FR-PHARM-001.3 Medication Dispensing

Authorized pharmacy staff shall be able to dispense one or more prescribed medications.

Each dispensing transaction shall include:

- Dispensing Number
- Dispensing Date & Time
- Dispensed By
- Medication
- Quantity Dispensed
- Batch Number (where applicable)
- Expiry Date (where applicable)
- Notes

---

## FR-PHARM-001.4 Partial Dispensing

The system shall support partial dispensing when the full prescribed quantity is unavailable.

The system shall automatically maintain:

- Remaining Quantity
- Outstanding Dispensing
- Dispensing History

Organizations may enable or disable partial dispensing.

---

## FR-PHARM-001.5 Dispensing Status

Each prescription shall support the following lifecycle:

- Pending
- Partially Dispensed
- Fully Dispensed
- Cancelled
- Expired

Status changes shall occur automatically where applicable.

---

## FR-PHARM-001.6 Inventory Synchronization

Successful dispensing shall automatically:

- Deduct inventory.
- Update stock balances.
- Record inventory movement.
- Maintain batch traceability.

All inventory updates shall execute within the same transaction.

---

## FR-PHARM-001.7 Billing Integration

Where configured, dispensed medications shall automatically generate billable invoice items within the Billing module.

Organizations may configure billing to occur:

- At Dispensing
- At Prescription Verification
- At Prescription Creation

---

## FR-PHARM-001.8 Dispensing History

The system shall maintain a complete dispensing history for each patient.

Users shall be able to review:

- Previous Medications
- Dispensing Dates
- Dispensing Quantities
- Pharmacist
- Related Encounter
- Associated Prescription

---

# 6. Validation Rules

The system shall validate:

- Patient identity.
- Prescription validity.
- Medication availability.
- User permissions.
- Dispensing eligibility.
- Duplicate dispensing attempts.

Validation failures shall prevent dispensing.

---

# 7. Business Rules

## BR-PHARM-001

Medications shall only be dispensed against valid authorized prescriptions unless organizational policy explicitly permits over-the-counter dispensing.

---

## BR-PHARM-002

Every dispensing transaction shall receive a unique dispensing number.

---

## BR-PHARM-003

Inventory quantities shall be updated immediately after successful dispensing.

---

## BR-PHARM-004

Dispensing records shall never be permanently deleted.

---

## BR-PHARM-005

Every dispensing action shall be fully auditable.

---

## BR-PHARM-006

Patient medication history shall remain permanently available for clinical review.

---

# 8. Non-Functional Requirements

The Pharmacy module shall:

- Support enterprise-scale dispensing operations.
- Maintain complete audit history.
- Support multi-branch organizations.
- Ensure transactional consistency with Inventory and Billing.
- Support future regulatory enhancements.
- Maintain high availability during dispensing operations.

---

# 9. Acceptance Criteria

The requirement shall be considered complete when:

- Electronic prescriptions can be retrieved.
- Prescription validation functions correctly.
- Medications can be dispensed successfully.
- Partial dispensing is supported.
- Inventory updates automatically.
- Billing integration functions correctly.
- Dispensing history is maintained.
- Audit records are generated automatically.

---

# 10. Architectural Notes

The Pharmacy module shall operate as a clinical service tightly integrated with EMR, Inventory, Billing, Reporting, Security, and Audit Trail.

Dispensing operations shall execute atomically to ensure consistency between prescription records, inventory balances, billing transactions, and patient medication history.

The architecture shall support future enhancements including e-Prescribing, barcode verification, medication reconciliation, controlled substance management, and interoperability standards without requiring structural redesign.

---

# FR-PHARM-002 — Medication Verification & Clinical Safety

**Document Classification:** Functional Requirement
**Priority:** Critical
**Status:** Approved Draft
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Business Objective

Provide a comprehensive medication verification framework that ensures patient safety by validating prescriptions, detecting medication-related risks, enforcing dispensing rules, and supporting pharmacists in delivering safe pharmaceutical care.

---

# 2. Scope

This requirement governs all clinical validation activities performed before medication dispensing, including allergy checking, interaction detection, duplicate therapy prevention, dosage validation, contraindication screening, and pharmacist intervention documentation.

---

# 3. Primary Actors

- Pharmacist
- Pharmacy Technician
- Physician (Read-Only)
- System Administrator

---

# 4. Preconditions

The following conditions shall be satisfied before medication verification:

- User is authenticated.
- Patient exists.
- Prescription exists.
- Medication catalog is available.
- Patient allergy information is accessible.
- Clinical medication database is available.

---

# 5. Functional Requirements

## FR-PHARM-002.1 Allergy Verification

The system shall automatically compare prescribed medications against the patient's documented allergies.

Potential allergy conflicts shall generate clinical alerts before dispensing.

---

## FR-PHARM-002.2 Drug Interaction Checking

The system shall evaluate prescribed medications against the patient's active medication list.

Potential interactions shall be classified by severity, including:

- Minor
- Moderate
- Major
- Contraindicated

Organizations may configure alert thresholds.

---

## FR-PHARM-002.3 Duplicate Therapy Detection

The system shall detect duplicate medications based on:

- Active Ingredient
- Therapeutic Class
- Duplicate Prescription

Potential duplicate therapies shall generate pharmacist alerts.

---

## FR-PHARM-002.4 Dosage Validation

The system shall verify prescribed dosages against configured medication references.

Validation may include:

- Minimum Dose
- Maximum Dose
- Frequency
- Duration
- Age-Based Dose
- Weight-Based Dose

---

## FR-PHARM-002.5 Contraindication Screening

The system shall identify medication contraindications based on available patient clinical information, including:

- Allergies
- Diagnoses
- Pregnancy Status (where recorded)
- Renal Impairment
- Hepatic Impairment

Additional contraindication rules may be configured in future releases.

---

## FR-PHARM-002.6 Pharmacist Intervention

Authorized pharmacists shall be able to document clinical interventions including:

- Clarification Request
- Prescription Modification Recommendation
- Patient Counseling
- Physician Communication
- Medication Substitution Recommendation

---

## FR-PHARM-002.7 Alert Override

Where organizational policy permits, authorized pharmacists may override clinical alerts.

Each override shall require:

- Override Reason
- User
- Date & Time
- Supporting Notes

All overrides shall be recorded in the audit trail.

---

## FR-PHARM-002.8 Verification History

The system shall maintain a complete medication verification history including:

- Clinical Alerts
- Pharmacist Actions
- Overrides
- Recommendations
- Dispensing Decision

---

# 6. Validation Rules

The system shall validate:

- Prescription completeness.
- Patient clinical data availability.
- Medication safety checks.
- Pharmacist permissions.
- Override authorization.

Validation failures shall prevent medication dispensing unless an authorized override is completed.

---

# 7. Business Rules

## BR-PHARM-007

Medication safety verification shall occur before every dispensing transaction.

---

## BR-PHARM-008

Critical clinical alerts shall require pharmacist review.

---

## BR-PHARM-009

Only authorized users may override medication safety alerts.

---

## BR-PHARM-010

Every override shall include a documented justification.

---

## BR-PHARM-011

Clinical intervention records shall never be permanently deleted.

---

## BR-PHARM-012

Medication verification activities shall be fully auditable.

---

# 8. Non-Functional Requirements

The medication verification engine shall:

- Execute safety checks with minimal latency.
- Support configurable clinical decision rules.
- Maintain complete audit history.
- Support enterprise scalability.
- Allow future integration with external medication knowledge bases.

---

# 9. Acceptance Criteria

The requirement shall be considered complete when:

- Allergy verification functions correctly.
- Drug interaction checking identifies configured risks.
- Duplicate therapies are detected.
- Dosage validation operates correctly.
- Contraindications generate appropriate alerts.
- Pharmacist interventions are documented.
- Alert overrides follow organizational policy.
- Audit records are generated for all verification activities.

---

# 10. Architectural Notes

The Medication Verification Engine shall operate as a reusable clinical decision support component shared between the Pharmacy and EMR modules.

The architecture shall support configurable medication knowledge bases, future interoperability standards, barcode-assisted verification, and advanced clinical decision support while maintaining transactional consistency and patient safety.

---

Provide a centralized medication master catalog that maintains standardized medication information, supports safe prescribing and dispensing, enables inventory synchronization, and ensures consistency across all clinical and financial workflows.

---

# 2. Scope

This requirement governs the creation, maintenance, organization, and lifecycle management of medications within the Pharmacy module.

---

# 3. Primary Actors

- Pharmacist
- Pharmacy Manager
- Inventory Manager
- System Administrator

---

- User is authenticated.
- User has medication management permissions.
- Pharmacy module is active.

---

## FR-PHARM-003.1 Medication Master Record

The system shall maintain a master record for every medication.

Each medication shall include, at minimum:

- Medication Code
- Generic Name
- Brand Name
- Scientific Name (Optional)
- Therapeutic Class
- Pharmacological Class
- Dosage Form
- Strength
- Unit of Measure
- Route of Administration
- Manufacturer
- Country of Origin (Optional)

- Analgesics
- Anti-inflammatory
- Cardiovascular
- Endocrine
- Gastrointestinal
- Respiratory
- Dermatology
- Ophthalmology
- Controlled Medications
- High-Alert Medications

Organizations may create additional categories.

---

## FR-PHARM-003.3 Medication Search

Authorized users shall be able to search medications using:

- Medication Code
- Generic Name
- Brand Name
- Active Ingredient
- Therapeutic Class

## FR-PHARM-003.5 Medication Information

Each medication record may include additional clinical information such as:

- Indications
- Contraindications

- Storage Instructions
- Patient Counseling Notes

## FR-PHARM-003.6 Medication Images

- Product Packaging
- Tablet/Capsule Appearance
- Bottle Label

---

## FR-PHARM-003.7 Medication Version History

The system shall preserve historical versions of medication records.

Users shall be able to review:

- Previous Values
- Updated Values
- Modified By
- Modification Date & Time

---

## FR-PHARM-003.8 Future Integration

The medication catalog shall support future integration with:

- National Drug Databases
- Regulatory Authorities
- Barcode Databases
- Drug Knowledge Bases
- External Pharmaceutical APIs

---

- Unique medication code.
- Required medication information.
- Valid classification.
- User permissions.

# 7. Business Rules

---

## BR-PHARM-014

---

## BR-PHARM-015

---

## BR-PHARM-016

---

## BR-PHARM-017

---

## BR-PHARM-018

The medication catalog shall:

- Provide fast search performance.
- Maintain complete audit history.
- Support configurable medication classifications.
- Support multilingual medication names.
- Support future interoperability standards.

---

- Medication classifications function correctly.
- Medication searches return accurate results.

- Historical versions are preserved.

# FR-PHARM-005 — Medication Returns & Disposal Management

**Document Classification:** Functional Requirement
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved Draft
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Business Objective

Provide a controlled framework for managing medication returns, expired medications, damaged stock, and disposal activities while ensuring patient safety, inventory accuracy, financial integrity, regulatory compliance, and complete auditability.

---

# 2. Scope

This requirement governs all medication return and disposal activities performed within the Pharmacy module, including:

- Patient medication returns
- Dispensing reversals
- Expired medications
- Damaged medications
- Recall management
- Medication disposal
- Inventory adjustments
- Billing reconciliation

---

# 3. Primary Actors

- Pharmacist
- Pharmacy Manager
- Inventory Manager
- Billing Officer
- System Administrator

---

# 4. Preconditions

The following conditions shall be satisfied before processing returns or disposal:

- User is authenticated.
- User has return/disposal permissions.
- Original dispensing transaction exists (for returns).
- Medication batch information is available where applicable.
- Organizational return policy permits the requested operation.

---

# 5. Functional Requirements

## FR-PHARM-005.1 Medication Return

Authorized users shall be able to process medication returns according to organizational policy.

Each return shall record:

- Return Number
- Original Dispensing Reference
- Medication
- Quantity Returned
- Return Reason
- Return Date & Time
- Processed By

---

## FR-PHARM-005.2 Return Validation

Before accepting a medication return, the system shall validate:

- Original dispensing transaction.
- Return eligibility period.
- Medication condition.
- Batch verification (where applicable).
- Organizational return policy.

Validation failures shall prevent return processing.

---

## FR-PHARM-005.3 Inventory Adjustment

Approved medication returns shall automatically update inventory by:

- Returning usable stock to inventory where permitted.
- Recording damaged or unusable items separately.
- Maintaining complete inventory movement history.

---

- Invoice Adjustment
- Partial Refund
- Credit Balance
- No Financial Adjustment

All billing updates shall remain traceable to the original dispensing transaction.

---

## FR-PHARM-005.5 Expired Medication Management

The system shall identify medications approaching or exceeding their expiry date.

Organizations shall be able to configure notification thresholds.

Expired medications shall not be available for dispensing.

---

## FR-PHARM-005.6 Damaged Medication Management

Authorized users shall record medications that are:

- Damaged
- Contaminated
- Recalled
- Unusable

Such medications shall immediately become unavailable for dispensing.

---

## FR-PHARM-005.7 Medication Disposal

The system shall support controlled medication disposal.

Each disposal record shall include:

- Disposal Number
- Medication
- Batch Number
- Quantity
- Disposal Reason
- Disposal Method
- Disposal Date
- Authorized By
- Witness (Optional)

---

## FR-PHARM-005.8 Return & Disposal History

The system shall maintain a permanent history of:

- Medication Returns
- Inventory Adjustments
- Disposal Activities
- Financial Adjustments
- Related Audit Events

---

# 6. Validation Rules

The system shall validate:

- User permissions.
- Medication eligibility.
- Batch information.
- Return policy.
- Disposal authorization.
- Inventory consistency.

Validation failures shall prevent completion of the operation.

---

---

---

## BR-PHARM-027

## BR-PHARM-028

# FR-PHARM-007 — Pharmacy Reports & Integration

**Document Classification:** Functional Requirement
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved Draft
**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Business Objective

Provide comprehensive pharmacy reporting and seamless integration capabilities that enable healthcare organizations to monitor pharmacy performance, medication utilization, dispensing activities, inventory movements, financial transactions, and regulatory compliance while maintaining enterprise-wide interoperability.

---

# 2. Scope

This requirement governs all pharmacy reporting, operational analytics, financial reporting, regulatory reporting, and integration with other LOUTAS Care modules and future external systems.

---

# 3. Primary Actors

- Pharmacist
- Pharmacy Manager
- Inventory Manager
- Finance Manager
- Clinic Manager
- System Administrator

---

# 4. Preconditions

The following conditions shall be satisfied before generating reports or exchanging data:

- User is authenticated.
- User has reporting or integration permissions.
- Pharmacy transactions exist.
- Integrated modules are operational where applicable.

---

# 5. Functional Requirements

## FR-PHARM-007.1 Pharmacy Dashboard

The system shall provide a configurable pharmacy dashboard displaying key operational indicators including:

- Total Prescriptions
- Total Dispensing Transactions
- Pending Prescriptions
- Partially Dispensed Prescriptions
- Daily Medication Utilization
- Low Stock Alerts
- Expired Medication Alerts
- Controlled Medication Activity

Dashboard widgets shall be configurable according to user roles.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.2 Dispensing Reports

The system shall generate reports including:

- Daily Dispensing Register
- Medication Dispensing History
- Dispensing by Pharmacist
- Dispensing by Physician
- Dispensing by Department
- Dispensing by Branch

Reports shall support configurable filtering and sorting.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.3 Medication Utilization Reports

The reporting engine shall provide analytical reports including:

- Most Frequently Dispensed Medications
- Medication Consumption Trends
- Therapeutic Class Analysis
- Generic vs Brand Utilization
- Controlled Medication Usage
- High-Cost Medication Analysis

---

## FR-PHARM-007.4 Regulatory Reports

The system shall generate reports required for pharmacy governance including:

- Controlled Medication Register
- Medication Disposal Register
- Medication Return Register
- Expired Medication Report
- Inventory Variance Report
- Regulatory Audit Report

Organizations may configure additional regulatory reports.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.5 Financial Reports

The Pharmacy module shall support financial reporting including:

- Pharmacy Revenue
- Medication Sales
- Dispensing Charges
- Refund Summary
- Billing Reconciliation
- Medication Cost Analysis

Financial reports shall remain synchronized with the Billing module.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.6 Module Integration

The Pharmacy module shall integrate with:

- EMR
- Billing
- Inventory
- Patient Management
- Reports
- Security
- Audit Trail

Integration shall occur through controlled service interfaces.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.7 External Integration

The Pharmacy module shall support future integration with:

- National Drug Databases
- Electronic Prescription Platforms
- Barcode Systems
- Pharmaceutical Suppliers
- Regulatory Authorities
- ERP Platforms

External interfaces shall be configurable.

---

## FR-PHARM-007.8 Report Export

Authorized users shall export reports in supported formats including:

- PDF
- Microsoft Excel
- CSV

Additional export formats may be introduced in future releases.

---

- User permissions.
- Report parameters.
- Date ranges.
- Branch access permissions.
- Data availability.
