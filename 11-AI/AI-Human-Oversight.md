# AI Human Oversight

## Purpose

This document defines the Human Oversight Framework for Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the **LOUTAS Care Platform**.

The purpose of this framework is to ensure that AI assists healthcare professionals without replacing human expertise, professional responsibility, or clinical decision-making.

All AI-generated outputs shall remain subject to appropriate human review based on their impact and associated risk.

---

# Objectives

The Human Oversight Framework aims to:

- Protect patient safety.
- Maintain clinician authority.
- Ensure accountability.
- Prevent unsafe automation.
- Support transparent AI usage.
- Define approval responsibilities.
- Establish escalation procedures.
- Comply with healthcare governance requirements.

---

# Guiding Principles

Human oversight shall be based on the following principles:

- Human-Centered AI
- Patient Safety First
- Clinical Accountability
- Transparency
- Explainability
- Professional Responsibility
- Least Automation Necessary
- Continuous Review

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Clinical AI Assistants
- Medical Summaries
- SOAP Note Generation
- AI Documentation Assistance
- AI Decision Support
- Prescription Assistance
- Laboratory Interpretation
- Radiology Assistance
- Administrative AI Services
- Future AI-enabled workflows

---

# Human Responsibility

AI is an assistive technology.

Healthcare professionals remain responsible for:

- Diagnosis
- Treatment decisions
- Medication prescribing
- Interpretation of clinical findings
- Patient communication
- Documentation approval
- Clinical judgement

Responsibility for patient care shall never be delegated to AI.

---

# Oversight Levels

## Level 1 — Informational Assistance

Examples:

- Administrative suggestions
- Documentation formatting
- Appointment summaries

Human review is recommended.

---

## Level 2 — Professional Assistance

Examples:

- Clinical summaries
- Suggested SOAP notes
- Draft referral letters
- Draft discharge summaries

Human review is mandatory before use.

---

## Level 3 — High-Risk Clinical Assistance

Examples:

- Clinical recommendations
- Medication suggestions
- Diagnostic support
- Treatment planning

Mandatory requirements:

- Human review
- Clinical approval
- Verification against patient data

AI recommendations shall not be applied automatically.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

The platform shall implement Human-in-the-Loop workflows where appropriate.

Typical workflow:

```
User Request
      │
      ▼
AI Processing
      │
      ▼
AI Recommendation
      │
      ▼
Human Review
      │
      ▼
Approve / Edit / Reject
      │
      ▼
Final Action
```

The reviewer shall have the ability to modify or reject AI-generated outputs.

---

# Approval Requirements

The following actions require explicit human approval:

- Clinical documentation finalization
- Prescription issuance
- Medical coding confirmation
- Referral approval
- Diagnostic interpretation
- Patient communication generated by AI
- Significant knowledge base updates

Approvals shall be recorded where required by organizational policy.

---

# AI Recommendation Display

AI-generated recommendations shall be clearly distinguishable from user-entered information.

The user interface should:

- Identify AI-generated content.
- Display confidence information where available.
- Allow editing before acceptance.
- Record acceptance or rejection.
- Preserve audit history.

Users shall not be misled into believing AI-generated content is authoritative.

---

# Override Capability

Authorized users shall be able to:

- Edit AI-generated text.
- Reject AI recommendations.
- Replace AI output.
- Disable AI assistance where permitted.

Human decisions shall always take precedence over AI outputs.

---

# Escalation

The platform shall support escalation when:

- AI confidence is low.
- Validation fails.
- Conflicting clinical information exists.
- Unsafe recommendations are detected.
- Critical patient conditions are identified.

Escalation may require review by a senior clinician or designated authority.

---

# Audit Requirements

The following events shall be auditable:

- AI recommendation generated
- Human review completed
- Recommendation accepted
- Recommendation modified
- Recommendation rejected
- Reviewer identity
- Timestamp
- AI model version
- Prompt version

Audit records shall comply with organizational retention policies.

---

# Training Requirements

Personnel using AI-assisted features shall receive training on:

- AI capabilities
- AI limitations
- Safe AI usage
- Clinical verification
- Security responsibilities
- Privacy obligations
- Incident reporting

Training records should be maintained where required.

---

# Performance Monitoring

Human oversight effectiveness shall be monitored through:

- Acceptance rate
- Modification rate
- Rejection rate
- Clinical review findings
- User feedback
- Safety incidents
- Documentation quality
- Audit observations

Monitoring results shall support continuous improvement.

---

# Incident Handling

If unsafe AI behavior is identified:

1. Stop use of the affected capability if necessary.
2. Record the incident.
3. Notify appropriate stakeholders.
4. Investigate the root cause.
5. Implement corrective actions.
6. Validate the solution.
7. Approve reactivation before returning to production.

---

# Governance

Human oversight processes shall be reviewed:

- Annually
- Following significant AI changes
- After safety incidents
- After regulatory updates
- Following major workflow changes

Governance updates shall be documented and approved.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|

| AI Engineering Team | Improve AI behavior |
| Security Team | Review security implications |
