# Artificial Intelligence Repository

The **AI** repository contains the official documentation governing the design, development, deployment, operation, monitoring, and governance of Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the **LOUTAS Care Platform**.

Artificial Intelligence is considered a core platform capability and shall operate under strict governance to ensure patient safety, data privacy, regulatory compliance, explainability, and continuous improvement.

This repository serves as the authoritative reference for all AI-related architecture, policies, models, prompts, evaluation methods, deployment strategies, and operational procedures.

---

# Objectives

This repository aims to:

- Standardize AI implementation across the platform.
- Define AI architecture and governance.
- Ensure responsible and ethical AI usage.
- Protect patient privacy and sensitive healthcare information.
- Improve clinical decision support.
- Establish AI lifecycle management.
- Provide operational guidance for AI services.
- Maintain traceability and auditability.
- Support future AI expansion.

---

# Scope

This repository applies to all AI capabilities integrated into the LOUTAS Care platform, including:

- Clinical Decision Support
- Medical Documentation Assistance
- AI-Powered Summarization
- Intelligent Search
- Prompt-Based Assistants
- OCR Services
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Speech-to-Text
- AI Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)
- AI Reporting
- Future Machine Learning Services

# Governance

AI capabilities shall be governed through:

- Architecture Reviews
- Security Reviews
- Clinical Validation
- Prompt Governance
- Knowledge Base Governance
- Model Versioning
- Risk Assessment
- Human Approval Processes

No AI capability shall be deployed to production without completing the required governance and validation activities.
