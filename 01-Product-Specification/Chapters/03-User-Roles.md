# Purpose

This document defines the official user roles supported by the LOUTAS Care platform.

User roles determine responsibilities, permissions, workflow participation, and access to system functions.

Detailed permissions are defined separately within the Security and RBAC documentation.

---

# Core User Roles

## 1. System Administrator

Responsibilities:

* Configure the platform.
* Manage users and roles.
* Manage security settings.
* Configure system parameters.
* Monitor system health.

---

## 2. Clinic Owner

Responsibilities:

* Review business performance.
* Monitor financial reports.
* Monitor operational KPIs.
* Approve strategic decisions.
* Access executive dashboards.

---

## 3. Branch Manager

Responsibilities:

* Manage branch operations.
* Supervise daily activities.
* Review appointments.
* Monitor staff performance.
* Review branch reports.

---

## 4. Receptionist

Responsibilities:

* Register patients.
* Schedule appointments.
* Check patients in.
* Update demographic information.
* Manage appointment calendar.

---

## 5. Physician

Responsibilities:

* Review patient history.
* Conduct consultations.
* Document clinical encounters.
* Create diagnoses.
* Prescribe medications.
* Order laboratory tests.
* Order radiology studies.
* Complete patient visits.

---

## 6. Nurse

Responsibilities:

* Record vital signs.
* Prepare patients.
* Assist physicians.
* Document nursing observations.
* Support clinical workflow.

---

## 7. Cashier

Responsibilities:

* Create invoices.
* Collect payments.
* Issue receipts.
* Process refunds (when authorized).
* Close financial transactions.

---

## 8. Laboratory Staff

Responsibilities:

* Receive laboratory orders.
* Record laboratory results.
* Update order status.
* Publish approved results.

---

## 9. Radiology Staff

Responsibilities:

* Manage imaging requests.
* Upload imaging reports.
* Complete radiology workflows.

---

## 10. Pharmacist

Responsibilities:

* Dispense medications.
* Verify prescriptions.
* Manage medication inventory.
* Record dispensing history.

---

## 11. Patient

Responsibilities:

* View personal information.
* Review appointments.
* Access reports when permitted.
* Receive communications.

---

# Role Management Principles

* Least Privilege Principle.
* Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
* Separation of Duties.
* Full Audit Logging.
* Permission inheritance only through approved role definitions.
