# Purpose

This document defines the official functional modules of the LOUTAS Care platform.

Every screen, workflow, API, database object, report, and business process shall belong to one of these modules.

This document serves as the master functional map of the system.

---

# Core Platform Modules

## 1. Dashboard

Provides personalized dashboards, KPIs, alerts, operational summaries, and quick access to daily activities.

---

## 2. Reception & Front Desk

Supports patient registration, appointment booking, patient check-in, queue management, and demographic updates.

---

## 3. Appointment Management

Manages physician schedules, calendars, appointment lifecycle, waiting lists, confirmations, cancellations, and visit status.

---

## 4. Patient Management

Maintains patient demographic information, identifiers, medical history references, and longitudinal patient records.

---

## 5. Electronic Medical Record (EMR)

Supports clinical documentation, diagnoses, medications, allergies, vital signs, progress notes, clinical history, laboratory requests, radiology requests, and treatment plans.

---

## 6. Billing & Finance

Supports invoices, payments, discounts, insurance processing, memberships, cashier operations, financial reporting, and revenue tracking.

---

## 7. Pharmacy

Supports medication catalog management, dispensing, prescription processing, medication inventory, and pharmacy workflows.

---

## 8. Laboratory

Supports laboratory orders, specimen tracking, result entry, verification, and laboratory reporting.

---

## 9. Radiology

Supports imaging requests, scheduling, reporting, image references, and radiology workflow management.

---

## 10. Inventory & Supply Chain

Supports inventory items, stock movements, purchasing, suppliers, warehouses, batch tracking, and consumption management.

---

## 11. Reporting & Analytics

Provides operational, financial, clinical, and executive reports together with dashboards and business intelligence.

---

## 12. Administration

Supports user management, branches, clinics, departments, configuration, master data, system settings, and organizational management.

---

## 13. Security & Access Control

Supports authentication, authorization, RBAC, audit logs, password policies, security monitoring, and compliance.

---

## 14. AI Services

Supports AI-assisted clinical documentation, intelligent search, workflow assistance, decision support, summarization, and future AI capabilities under physician supervision.

---

# Module Principles

* Every feature belongs to one module.
* Modules communicate through approved business workflows.
* Modules remain loosely coupled.
* Shared master data shall be centrally managed.
* Future modules require governance approval before introduction.
