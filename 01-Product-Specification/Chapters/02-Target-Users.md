# Purpose

This document identifies the organizations and individuals for whom the LOUTAS Care platform is designed.

Understanding the target users ensures that every feature, workflow, and user interface supports real operational needs.

---

# Primary Customers

LOUTAS Care is designed primarily for healthcare organizations providing outpatient medical services.

These include:

* Private outpatient clinics
* Multi-specialty medical centers
* Dental clinics
* Ophthalmology centers
* Endoscopy centers
* Day surgery centers
* Specialized medical practices

---

# Primary System Users

The platform is intended to support the daily work of:

* Physicians
* Receptionists
* Nurses
* Cashiers
* Laboratory staff
* Radiology staff
* Pharmacists
* Clinic Managers
* Branch Managers
* System Administrators

---

# Secondary Users

The platform also provides value to:

* Patients
* Clinic owners
* Financial managers
* Quality management teams
* Technical support teams

---

# User Characteristics

The platform shall be designed for users with different levels of technical experience.

Key design principles include:

* Simple and intuitive interfaces.
* Minimal training requirements.
* Fast task completion.
* Arabic-first user experience with multilingual support.
* Responsive design across supported devices.

---

# Accessibility Goals

LOUTAS Care should support:

* Clear navigation.
* Readable interfaces.
* Consistent layouts.
* Efficient workflows.
* Reduced cognitive load for busy healthcare professionals.

---

# Future User Expansion

Future releases may introduce additional user groups, including:

* Insurance companies
* Corporate healthcare programs
* Home healthcare providers
* Telemedicine providers
* Government healthcare integrations

These groups will be documented as the platform evolves.
