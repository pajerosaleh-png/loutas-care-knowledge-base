# Purpose

This document defines the official scope of the LOUTAS Care platform.

It establishes what the platform is intended to deliver, the problems it aims to solve, and the boundaries within which the product will evolve.

All future development shall align with the scope defined in this document.

---

# Product Overview

LOUTAS Care is a cloud-based Healthcare Information System (HIS) designed primarily for private outpatient clinics, specialty medical centers, and day-care facilities.

The platform provides healthcare professionals with a secure, integrated, and intelligent environment for managing clinical, administrative, and financial workflows while maintaining a complete longitudinal electronic medical record for every patient.

---

# Product Objectives

The primary objectives of LOUTAS Care are:

* Improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare delivery.
* Simplify clinical and administrative workflows.
* Maintain a complete and lifelong electronic medical record.
* Reduce paperwork and duplicated medical information.
* Support physicians in making informed clinical decisions.
* Improve communication between healthcare providers and patients.
* Increase operational efficiency for healthcare organizations.
* Support future AI-assisted clinical workflows under physician supervision.

---

# Target Market

The initial target market includes:

* Private outpatient clinics.
* Multi-specialty medical centers.
* Day surgery centers.
* Dental clinics.
* Ophthalmology centers.
* Endoscopy centers.
* Other ambulatory healthcare providers.

Future expansion may include additional healthcare sectors as defined in the product roadmap.

---

# Core Business Domains

The platform is expected to support the following high-level domains:

* Patient Management
* Appointment Management
* Electronic Medical Records (EMR)
* Billing and Financial Management
* Clinical Documentation
* Laboratory Integration
* Radiology Integration
* Pharmacy Management
* Inventory Management
* Reporting and Analytics
* Administration and Security
* AI-Assisted Clinical Support

Each domain will be specified in dedicated documentation.

---

# Scope Principles

The product scope shall always follow these principles:

* Patient safety comes first.
* Clinical workflows take priority over administrative convenience.
* Security and privacy are mandatory.
* Documentation precedes implementation.
* Product evolution shall be controlled through governance and approved documentation.

---

# Scope Control

Any proposed functionality outside this document shall be evaluated through the official governance process before implementation.

Major scope changes require documentation review and approval.
