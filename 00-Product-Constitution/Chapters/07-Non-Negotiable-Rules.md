# Chapter 05 — Non-Negotiable Rules

---

## Introduction

The following rules define the permanent commitments of the LOUTAS Care platform.

These commitments are considered constitutional principles and shall not be violated without a formal architectural review and executive approval.

Every future feature, architectural decision, workflow, and AI capability must comply with these rules.

---

# Rule 1 — Patient Safety Comes First

No feature, workflow, or business requirement shall compromise patient safety.

Patient well-being always has priority over operational convenience or commercial interests.

---

# Rule 2 — Patient Data Must Be Protected

Patient medical information is confidential.

Security, privacy, access control, encryption, auditing, and responsible data handling are mandatory requirements throughout the platform.

---

# Rule 3 — Physicians Remain the Decision Makers

Artificial Intelligence and software recommendations are intended to assist healthcare professionals.

Clinical responsibility and medical decisions always remain with licensed healthcare providers.

---

# Rule 4 — No Hidden Business Logic

Critical healthcare workflows must be transparent, documented, auditable, and understandable.

The platform shall avoid undocumented behavior that may affect patient care or financial operations.

---

# Rule 5 — Every Important Action Must Be Traceable

Critical operations shall be recorded through audit logs to ensure accountability, transparency, and regulatory compliance.

---

# Rule 6 — Standards Before Shortcuts

No implementation shall bypass approved standards, governance policies, security requirements, or architectural decisions for the sake of speed.

Long-term quality always takes priority over temporary shortcuts.

---

# Rule 7 — Continuous Improvement Without Breaking Stability

LOUTAS Care shall continuously evolve while preserving system stability, backward compatibility where appropriate, and the integrity of patient information.

Innovation shall never come at the expense of reliability.

---

## Closing Statement

These rules represent the constitutional boundaries of the LOUTAS Care platform.

They are intended to preserve patient trust, protect healthcare professionals, ensure engineering discipline, and guide the long-term evolution of the platform.

Every contributor is expected to understand, respect, and uphold these commitments.
