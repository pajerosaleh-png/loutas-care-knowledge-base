# Purpose

This document defines the governance principles for reviewing user experience (UX) throughout the LOUTAS Care platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that user interfaces are evaluated consistently before implementation and throughout the product lifecycle, promoting usability, efficiency, maintainability, and alignment with enterprise architecture.

This document establishes governance principles only and does not prescribe implementation-specific review methodologies, testing tools, or organizational structures.

---

# Scope

This document applies to:

- New user interfaces
- Existing interface enhancements
- Shared platform components
- Business modules
- Clinical workflows
- Administrative workflows
- Future product features

---

# Architectural Objectives

UX reviews shall support:

- Usability
- Consistency
- Efficiency
- Accessibility
- Maintainability
- Enterprise governance

UX evaluation should improve user experience without compromising business requirements or architectural consistency.

---

# Review Principles

UX reviews shall follow these principles:

- Reviews should occur throughout the product lifecycle.
- Reviews should consider business workflows.
- Reviews should maintain consistency with established design principles.
- Recommendations should be documented.
- Approved improvements should follow change governance.

---

# Review Scope

UX reviews may evaluate:

- Navigation
- Workflow efficiency
- Information presentation
- Data entry experience
- User feedback
- Visual consistency
- Accessibility
- Overall usability

The specific review scope shall depend on the business context.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Reviews should consider:

- Task completion efficiency
- User understanding
- Workflow continuity
- Consistency
- Error prevention
- Learnability
- Overall user satisfaction

Specific evaluation methods are implementation decisions.

---

# Review Outcomes

UX reviews may result in:

- Approval
- Improvement recommendations
- Design revisions
- Usability enhancements
- Documentation updates

Implementation priorities are determined through product governance.

---

# Continuous Improvement

UX should be reviewed periodically to identify opportunities for improving user experience while preserving architectural consistency and business objectives.

Improvement activities should be documented and managed through approved governance processes.

---

# Governance

UX reviews shall:

- Follow enterprise architecture.
- Respect approved design principles.
- Consider business requirements.
- Support long-term maintainability.
- Preserve platform consistency.
