# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the strategic product roadmap for the **LOUTAS Care Platform**.

It describes the planned evolution of the product, major business capabilities, platform priorities, and long-term objectives. The roadmap aligns business strategy with product development while providing guidance for future planning.

---

# Vision

LOUTAS Care aims to become the leading cloud-based outpatient healthcare platform in the Middle East and North Africa, delivering an integrated, intelligent, secure, and patient-centered healthcare ecosystem.

The platform will enable clinics to manage clinical, administrative, financial, operational, and AI-assisted workflows through a unified solution.

---

# Product Goals

The roadmap supports the following strategic goals:

- Improve clinician productivity.
- Simplify outpatient operations.
- Enhance patient experience.
- Enable data-driven decision making.
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Build a scalable SaaS platform.
- Support multi-clinic healthcare networks.
- Expand into regional healthcare markets.

# Product Evolution

## Phase 1 — Core Platform Foundation

### Objectives

Establish a stable and secure outpatient management platform.

### Major Deliverables

- Authentication & Authorization
- Multi-Clinic Support
- User Management
- Reception
- Patient Registration
- Appointment Management
- Patient Journey
- Electronic Medical Record (EMR)
- Billing Foundation
- Reporting Framework

### Success Indicators

- Stable production platform
- Complete outpatient workflow
- Secure multi-user operation
