# LOUTAS Care AI Engineering Framework

Welcome to the Artificial Intelligence Engineering Workspace for the LOUTAS Care Platform.

This workspace provides the governance, standards, operational procedures, and AI profiles required to ensure that all AI participants contribute consistently, safely, and professionally throughout the software engineering lifecycle.

---

# Mission

Establish a governed, transparent, and scalable Artificial Intelligence Engineering Framework that supports enterprise software development while protecting architecture, patient safety, and product quality.

---

# Objectives

- Ensure consistent AI behaviour.
- Protect enterprise architecture.
- Support evidence-based engineering.
- Improve software quality.
- Minimise technical debt.
- Promote continuous improvement.
- Maintain a Single Source of Truth.
