# Purpose

This document records technical, architectural, documentation, and operational improvements that have been intentionally postponed.

The objective is to ensure that deferred work remains visible, traceable, and planned for future implementation.

Technical debt should always be managed—not ignored.

# Technical Debt Principles

* Technical debt may be accepted only when documented.
* Every technical debt item shall have a planned resolution.
* High-priority debt shall be reviewed regularly.
* Technical debt shall never compromise patient safety, security, or system integrity.
