# Active Architectural Policies

* Documentation before implementation.
* Architecture before coding.
* Every major decision requires documentation.
* Patient safety has priority over business convenience.
* AI supports healthcare professionals but never replaces clinical judgment.
* Repository organization follows enterprise knowledge management principles.
