# Purpose

This document identifies, evaluates, and tracks risks that may affect the successful delivery of the LOUTAS Care platform.

The objective is to proactively manage risks before they become project issues.

# Risk Management Principles

* Risks shall be identified early.
* Every significant risk shall have a mitigation plan.
* Risks shall be reviewed periodically.
* Closed risks shall remain documented for historical reference.

---

# Notes

The Risk Register is a living document and shall be updated whenever new strategic, technical, security, or operational risks are identified.
