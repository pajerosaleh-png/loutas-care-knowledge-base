# Purpose

This document defines the governance process for reviewing, approving, and maintaining documentation within the LOUTAS Care Knowledge Base.

Its objective is to ensure that all documentation is technically accurate, architecturally consistent, and formally approved before becoming an authoritative project reference.

---

# Scope

This policy applies to all documentation maintained within the repository, including governance, architecture, business domains, clinical, billing, database, security, platform, UI/UX, development, AI, standards, and enterprise management.

---

# Review Objectives

The review process shall ensure that documentation is:

- Accurate
- Complete
- Consistent
- Traceable
- Maintainable
- Aligned with approved architecture

---

# Review Principles

Document reviews shall follow these principles:

- Independent review where practical.
- Evidence-based validation.
- Consistency with existing governance.
- Clear documentation of review outcomes.
- Controlled approval process.

---

# Review Workflow

The standard review workflow is:

1. Document Authoring
2. Technical Review
3. Architecture Review
4. Business Review (where applicable)
5. Approval
6. Publication
7. Ongoing Maintenance

Steps may be adapted where appropriate, provided governance requirements are maintained.

---

# Review Responsibilities

Typical responsibilities include:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Document Owner | Prepare and maintain the document |
| Technical Reviewer | Validate technical accuracy |
| Domain Owner | Validate business or clinical content |
| Enterprise Architecture | Validate architectural consistency |
| Repository Maintainer | Publish approved documentation |

One individual may perform multiple roles where appropriate, provided governance integrity is maintained.

---

# Approval Criteria

Before approval, a document should:

- Be technically complete.
- Be internally consistent.
- Follow the approved document template.
- Use the correct document identifier.
- Include required metadata.
- Reference related documentation where applicable.

---

# Changes After Approval

Approved documents may be revised when:

- Business requirements change.
- Architecture changes.
- Regulatory requirements change.
- Errors are identified.
- Governance improvements are required.

All revisions shall be recorded in the Revision History section.

---

# Review Frequency

Documents should be reviewed:

- During major project milestones.
- Before major releases.
- Following significant architectural changes.
- When business processes change.

Additional reviews may be conducted as required.

---

# Repository Integrity

Reviews shall verify:

- Cross-reference accuracy.
- Document numbering compliance.
- Metadata consistency.
- Template compliance.
- Absence of duplicate or conflicting documentation.

---

# Compliance

No document shall be treated as an authoritative project reference until it has completed the applicable review and approval process.
