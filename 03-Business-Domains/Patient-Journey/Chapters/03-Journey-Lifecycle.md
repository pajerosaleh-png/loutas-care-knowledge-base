# Journey Lifecycle

Every Patient Journey within the LOUTAS Care platform follows a standardized lifecycle to ensure consistent, safe, and coordinated healthcare delivery.

The lifecycle begins when the patient first requests a healthcare service and ends only after all clinical and administrative activities related to that visit have been completed.

## Standard Lifecycle

1. Patient Identification
2. Patient Registration (if required)
3. Appointment Scheduling
4. Patient Check-In
5. Clinical Assessment
6. Consultation
7. Clinical Documentation (EMR)
8. Orders and Investigations (Laboratory / Radiology / Procedures)
9. Diagnosis and Treatment Plan
10. Prescription and Clinical Instructions
11. Billing and Payment
12. Patient Check-Out
13. Follow-Up (if required)
14. Journey Completion

Each stage represents a controlled business process with clearly defined responsibilities, validation rules, and transition criteria.

The completion of one stage enables the patient to proceed to the next authorized stage within the healthcare process.

Exceptional situations, including appointment cancellation, patient no-show, emergency workflows, or administrative corrections, follow dedicated business rules defined in their respective modules.

The Patient Journey Lifecycle provides the operational foundation for all clinical, administrative, and financial workflows within the LOUTAS Care platform.
