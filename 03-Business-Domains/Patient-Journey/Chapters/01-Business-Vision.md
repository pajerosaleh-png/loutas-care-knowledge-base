# Business Vision

The Patient Journey is the central business concept of the LOUTAS Care platform.

Every interaction between a patient and the healthcare organization is managed as a structured and traceable journey rather than as isolated activities.

A Patient Journey begins when the patient first interacts with the healthcare organization and continues through every operational and clinical stage until the journey is completed.

Each journey connects all related activities into one continuous healthcare process, including registration, appointments, consultation, clinical documentation, investigations, prescriptions, billing, and follow-up.

The purpose of the Patient Journey is to ensure continuity of care, improve coordination between healthcare professionals, reduce operational errors, and provide a complete view of the patient's experience.

The Patient Journey serves as the operational backbone of the LOUTAS Care platform and provides a unified framework that connects all business modules.

Every operational module within the platform contributes to creating, updating, monitoring, or completing a Patient Journey.
