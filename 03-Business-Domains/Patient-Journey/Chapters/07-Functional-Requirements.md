# Functional Requirements

## FR-PJ-001 — Create Patient Journey

The system shall create a new Patient Journey for every new patient visit according to approved business workflows.

---

## FR-PJ-002 — Unique Journey Identifier

The system shall assign a unique identifier to every Patient Journey.

---

## FR-PJ-003 — Track Journey State

The system shall continuously maintain the current operational state of each Patient Journey.

---

## FR-PJ-004 — Track Operational Ownership

The system shall maintain the current operational owner responsible for the Patient Journey.

---

## FR-PJ-005 — Record Journey Timeline

The system shall record all significant events performed during the Patient Journey in chronological order.

---

## FR-PJ-006 — Associate Clinical Orders

The system shall associate every clinical order with its originating Patient Journey.

---

## FR-PJ-007 — Support Internal, External and Referral Services

The system shall support Internal, External, and Referral fulfillment methods for all authorized clinical services.

---

## FR-PJ-008 — Maintain Clinical Context

The system shall preserve the clinical context submitted with every clinical order throughout its lifecycle.

---

## FR-PJ-009 — Support Cross-Module Integration

The Patient Journey shall provide the operational link between Reception, Appointment, EMR, Clinical Orders, Billing, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Radiology, and all future clinical modules.

---

## FR-PJ-010 — Complete Patient Journey

The system shall allow completion of the Patient Journey only after all mandatory business requirements have been satisfied according to organizational policies.

---

## FR-PJ-011 — Audit All Journey Activities

The system shall maintain a complete audit trail for every Patient Journey.

---

## FR-PJ-012 — Journey History

The system shall preserve completed Patient Journeys as part of the patient's permanent healthcare history.
