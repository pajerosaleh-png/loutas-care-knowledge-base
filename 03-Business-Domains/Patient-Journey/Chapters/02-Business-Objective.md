# Business Objective

The Patient Journey is designed to provide a standardized, coordinated, and patient-centered operational workflow across the entire healthcare organization.

Its primary objectives are to:

* Ensure that every patient follows a clear and traceable healthcare journey.
* Maintain continuity of care throughout all clinical and administrative stages.
* Support healthcare professionals with accurate, timely, and complete patient information.
* Reduce operational delays, duplicated work, and unnecessary administrative tasks.
* Improve communication and coordination between all departments involved in patient care.
* Enhance patient safety through standardized processes and controlled transitions between stages.
* Provide complete operational visibility for management and quality improvement.
* Establish a single operational framework that integrates all business modules within the LOUTAS Care platform.

The Patient Journey enables every authorized healthcare professional to understand the patient's current stage, previous activities, and next required action, ensuring efficient and consistent healthcare delivery.
