# Ownership

The Patient Journey shall always have one clearly identified operational owner.

The operational owner is the department or role currently responsible for performing the next authorized activity within the patient's journey.

Ownership changes only through approved business workflows and shall be recorded in the audit history.

## Standard Ownership Flow

| Journey Stage          | Primary Owner |
| ---------------------- | ------------- |
| Patient Registration   | Reception     |
| Appointment Scheduling | Reception     |
| Patient Check-In       | Reception     |
| Consultation           | Physician     |
| Clinical Documentation | Physician     |
| Laboratory Orders      | Laboratory    |
| Radiology Orders       | Radiology     |
| Pharmacy Dispensing    | Pharmacy      |
| Billing and Payment    | Cashier       |
| Follow-Up Coordination | Reception     |
| Journey Completion     | System        |

## Ownership Principles

* Only one operational owner shall exist at any point in time.
* Ownership shall transfer only after successful completion of the current stage.
* Ownership transfer shall follow approved business rules.
* Every ownership transfer shall be recorded in the audit history.
* Ownership identifies operational responsibility and does not replace access permissions.

Administrative supervisors may monitor any Patient Journey according to their assigned permissions without becoming the operational owner.
