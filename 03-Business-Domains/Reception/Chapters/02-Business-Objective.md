# Business Objective

The Reception Business Domain is responsible for ensuring that every Patient Journey begins accurately, efficiently, and consistently.

Its primary objectives are to:

* Verify patient identity before initiating any healthcare service.
* Register new patients and maintain accurate demographic information.
* Manage appointments across all supported communication channels.
* Initiate the Patient Journey according to approved business workflows.
* Coordinate patient arrival, check-in, and queue management.
* Verify administrative requirements before clinical care begins.
* Support insurance, corporate, membership, and self-pay workflows.
* Ensure that required patient information is complete before transferring responsibility to the next operational owner.
* Reduce administrative delays and duplicate registrations.
* Improve patient experience through organized and standardized reception processes.
* Provide operational visibility for healthcare staff and management.
* Maintain data quality, consistency, and traceability throughout the reception process.

The Reception Business Domain shall establish a reliable operational foundation that enables clinical departments to begin patient care efficiently and safely.
