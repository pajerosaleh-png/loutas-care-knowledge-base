# Reception Services

The Reception Business Domain provides the operational services required to initiate and coordinate the Patient Journey before clinical care begins.

## Core Services

### Patient Management

* Search Existing Patient
* Register New Patient
* Update Patient Demographic Information
* Verify Patient Identity
* View Patient Profile

---

### Appointment Management

* Create Appointment
* Reschedule Appointment
* Cancel Appointment
* Confirm Appointment
* Manage Appointment Queue

---

### Patient Arrival

* Patient Check-In
* Verify Arrival
* Assign Queue Position
* Notify Clinical Staff
* Transfer Patient to Next Operational Stage

---

### Journey Management

* Create Patient Journey
* View Current Journey Status
* View Journey History
* Transfer Operational Ownership

---

### Administrative Services

* Verify Administrative Requirements
* Record Administrative Notes
* Print Visit Documents
* Print Patient Labels
* Print Queue Ticket

---

### Communication Services

* Contact Patient
* Send Appointment Reminder
* Send Visit Notification
* Receive Appointment Requests
* Manage Communication History

---

### Future Capabilities

The Reception Business Domain is architected to support future operational services, including:

* Insurance Eligibility Verification
* Membership Verification
* Corporate Contract Validation
* Online Self Check-In
* Self-Service Kiosk
* Mobile Patient Check-In
* Digital Consent Collection
* Integration with National Healthcare Platforms

Future capabilities shall integrate without changing the core Reception architecture.
