# Operational Workflow

The Reception Business Domain manages the operational activities that initiate and coordinate the Patient Journey before clinical care begins.

The standard operational workflow is as follows:

## Step 1 — Patient Contact

The patient initiates contact through one of the supported communication channels.

Examples include:

* Front Desk
* Call Center
* Online Booking
* Mobile Application
* Patient Portal
* Future Digital Channels

---

## Step 2 — Patient Identification

The system identifies whether the patient already exists.

Possible outcomes:

* Existing Patient
* New Patient

---

## Step 3 — Patient Registration or Verification

If the patient is new:

* Register patient.
* Verify required demographic information.

If the patient already exists:

* Verify identity.
* Review and update demographic information if required.

---

## Step 4 — Appointment Management

The Reception Business Domain performs one of the following actions:

* Create Appointment
* Reschedule Appointment
* Confirm Appointment
* Cancel Appointment
* Walk-In Registration

---

## Step 5 — Administrative Verification

Verify all operational requirements before clinical care begins.

Examples may include:

* Required patient information
* Administrative completeness
* Future eligibility verification workflows

---

## Step 6 — Patient Check-In

Upon patient arrival:

* Confirm attendance.
* Perform check-in.
* Assign queue position where applicable.
* Notify the next operational owner.

---

## Step 7 — Patient Journey Initiation

The Patient Journey is initiated according to approved business workflows.

Operational ownership is transferred to the next responsible department.

---

## Step 8 — Operational Handover

Reception completes its operational responsibilities and transfers the Patient Journey to the appropriate clinical or operational department.

Examples include:

* Physician
* Nursing
* Laboratory
* Radiology
* Other authorized departments

---

# Workflow Principles

* Every workflow shall be traceable.
* Every workflow step shall be auditable.
* Every handover shall transfer operational ownership.
* Every workflow shall remain linked to the Patient Journey.
* Workflow execution shall be independent of the communication channel.
