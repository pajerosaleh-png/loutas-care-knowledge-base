# Business Vision

The Reception Business Domain is the operational gateway to the LOUTAS Care platform.

Its purpose is to initiate, coordinate, and manage the beginning of every Patient Journey through standardized administrative processes, regardless of the communication channel.

Reception ensures that every patient enters the healthcare organization through a controlled, consistent, and traceable workflow.

The Reception Business Domain supports multiple communication channels including front desk services, call center operations, online booking, mobile applications, patient portals, and future digital services while maintaining a unified operational process.

Reception serves as the coordination point between patients, healthcare professionals, administrative staff, and clinical departments before clinical care begins.

Its mission is to provide an efficient, patient-centered, and organized start to every healthcare encounter while ensuring data quality, operational readiness, and continuity of care.
