# Appointment Business Domain

## Overview

The Appointment Business Domain defines the business concepts, workflows, rules, and architectural principles governing appointment management within the LOUTAS Care platform.

This repository serves as the authoritative business reference for scheduling, appointment lifecycle management, patient flow, and interactions with other business domains.

---

## Scope

This repository covers:

- Appointment Business Vision
- Appointment Lifecycle
- Appointment States
- Business Rules
- Functional Requirements
- Business Integration
- Architecture Review

---

## Objectives

- Standardize appointment management across all clinics.
- Support scalable scheduling workflows.
- Ensure consistent patient flow.
- Define business ownership and responsibilities.
- Provide a foundation for future solution architecture.
